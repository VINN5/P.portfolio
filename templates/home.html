AI · NGO Governance

ReliefIQ

An AI knowledge and governance assistant for NGO staff. It answers questions only from the organisation's own policy documents, cites every claim, and says so when it can't answer instead of guessing.

  • Grounded, cited answers. Hybrid retrieval over a private document library using pgvector, with High / Medium / Low confidence scoring.
  • Citation enforcement. A response without verifiable citations is rejected server-side, not just discouraged in the prompt.
  • Role-based access. Field staff, manager and admin tiers enforced at the API, with document-level restrictions and version history.
  • Gap and conflict detection. Compares donor requirements against internal policies and flags what is covered, partial, missing or contradictory.
  • Spreadsheet analysis. Excel and CSV files are ingested as structured tables, with exact, code-computed comparison between versions.
  • Built to be resilient and auditable. Gemini → Groq → Anthropic fallback chain, prompt-injection defence at ingestion, and a full admin audit log.

Observability · Incident Analysis

PulseOps

An evidence-first incident investigator for OpenTelemetry services. Given a captured window of metrics, change events and traces around a symptom, it works out what changed, where, and what most plausibly caused it, and shows its evidence.

  • Deterministic core first. A pipeline of ingest, change-point detection, event alignment, dependency graph and scoring produces ranked hypotheses and a timeline before any model is involved.
  • Change-point detection. A CUSUM detector over a fixed baseline emits metric-change evidence for each affected signal.
  • Dependency graph from real traces. The service graph is built only from spans that were actually observed, never assumed.
  • Decoy-aware ranking. Deploys and config changes are aligned to the symptom and scored with a visible feature breakdown, tested on synthetic scenarios such as connection-pool exhaustion and a decoy deploy.
  • Optional model step. Hypotheses can be drafted by Anthropic or any OpenAI-compatible provider (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter), with citation checking, redaction, bounded retries and a deterministic fallback.
  • Documented decisions. Architecture choices are recorded as decision records alongside the code.
